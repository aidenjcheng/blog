EZ Physics helps students measure time in lab videos without manually estimating timestamps.

## Highlights

- Finds the exact time difference between two points in a video.
- Supports frame-by-frame movement with automatic FPS detection.
- Makes it easy to switch between videos.
- Saves past measurements for later review.

[View the source and demos on GitHub](https://github.com/aidenjcheng/physicsapp)
