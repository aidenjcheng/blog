BlindSight helps people navigate indoor spaces with voice input, on-device object tracking, depth sensing, and spatial audio.

## Highlights

- Uses ARKit depth and scene mesh data to understand nearby obstacles.
- Uses YOLOE through Core ML to track objects related to the user’s destination.
- Uses Gemini to choose useful secondary goals during navigation.
- Gives guidance through beeps, speech, and spatial audio.
